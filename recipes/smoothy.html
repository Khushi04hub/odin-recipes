Fruit Smoothy

Home

Description

A thick, refreshing blend of fruit and plant milk, with no dairy or honey.

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana (frozen is best)
  • 1 cup mixed fruit (mango, berries or pineapple)
  • 1 cup plant milk (soy, oat or almond)
  • 1 tbsp chia or flax seeds
  • 1-2 tsp maple syrup or dates (only if needed)
  • A few ice cubes

Steps

  1. Add the plant milk to a blender first, then the fruit, seeds and sweetener.
  2. Blend 45-60 seconds until smooth.
  3. Add ice if you want it thicker, and blend again briefly.
  4. Pour and serve immediately.
