Millet Porridge

Home

Description

A warm, nourishing breakfast bowl, naturally gluten-free and filling.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup millet (foxtail, pearl or finger millet flour all work)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup plant milk (almond, oat or coconut)
  • 2 tbsp jaggery, maple syrup or dates
  • ¼ tsp cardamom or cinnamon
  • Toppings: sliced banana, nuts, seeds, berries

Steps

  1. Rinse the millet and dry-roast it in a pot for 2-3 minutes until nutty.
  2. Add the water and bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer covered for 15-18 minutes.
  3. Pour in the plant milk and sweetener. Cook 5-7 minutes more, stirring, until creamy.
  4. Stir in the cardamom or cinnamon.
  5. Serve warm with your toppings.
