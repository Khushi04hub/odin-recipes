Thai Curry

Home

Description

A fragrant, creamy coconut curry with vegetables and tofu, served over rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2-3 tbsp vegan Thai curry paste (check the label, as many contain shrimp paste)
  • 1 can (400 ml) coconut milk
  • ½ cup vegetable stock or water
  • 200 g firm tofu, cubed
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables (bell pepper, broccoli, zucchini, carrot, baby corn)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Juice of ½ lime
  • Fresh basil or coriander
  • Cooked jasmine rice to serve

Steps

  1. Pan-fry the tofu in a little oil until golden. Set aside.
  2. In the same pan, fry the curry paste in 1 tbsp oil for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Pour in the coconut milk and stock and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add the harder vegetables (carrot, broccoli) and cook 5 minutes, then add the softer ones for 3-4 minutes.
  5. Stir in the tofu, soy sauce and sugar. Simmer 2 minutes.
  6. Finish with lime juice and fresh herbs. Serve over rice.
