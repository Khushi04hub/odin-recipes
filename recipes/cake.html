Carrot Cake

Home

Description

A moist, spiced loaf-style cake with grated carrot, walnuts and a tangy cashew-cream frosting.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup brown sugar or jaggery powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp nutmeg, ¼ tsp salt
  • 1½ cups grated carrot
  • ½ cup chopped walnuts (optional: raisins)
  • ½ cup neutral oil
  • ¾ cup plant milk (soy or oat)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice or apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C. Grease and line a loaf or 7-inch round tin.
  2. Stir the plant milk and lemon juice together and let it sit 5 minutes to curdle.
  3. Whisk in the oil, sugar and vanilla.
  4. Sift in the flour, baking powder, baking soda, spices and salt. Fold gently until just combined.
  5. Fold in the carrot and walnuts.
  6. Pour into the tin and bake 40-45 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
  7. Cool completely. Blend the frosting ingredients until smooth, then spread on top.
