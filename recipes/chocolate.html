Hot Chocolate

Home

Description

Rich, creamy and dairy-free, made with real cocoa.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups oat or soy milk
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 2-3 tbsp sugar or maple syrup
  • 30 g dairy-free dark chocolate, chopped
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt (optional: pinch of cinnamon)

Steps

  1. Whisk the cocoa, sugar and 3 tbsp of the milk in a saucepan into a smooth paste.
  2. Add the remaining milk and heat on medium-low, stirring, until steaming (don’t boil).
  3. Add the chopped chocolate, vanilla and salt, and whisk until melted and glossy.
  4. Pour into mugs and serve hot.
